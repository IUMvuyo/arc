# Demo script

Target length: about two minutes. Record with the tab in the foreground so the
animations run smoothly. Keep the cursor deliberate.

## Beat 1: the hook (0:00 to 0:12)

On the landing page, before touching anything.

> "What if an AI did not just answer your question, but noticed the actual shape of
> your week, and built you something true about it?"

Let the hero sit for a second. The oversized type and the fragment marquee do the
work.

## Beat 2: the read (0:12 to 1:05)

Paste a real week into the console, or tap the "a week of avoidance" prepared week.
Hit Build my site.

> "It is reading the week now. Not summarizing it. Looking for what recurred, what
> got avoided, and the one moment it turned."

Let the reading room play. Call out the through-line the moment it lands:

> "There it is. The whole week in one honest line."

Then the beats stream in. Do not talk over the through-line; let it breathe.

## Beat 3: the site (1:05 to 1:45)

The generated site loads on the title card. Scroll slowly.

> "This site did not exist a moment ago. The layout, the accent, the rhythm, all
> generated from this specific week."

Scroll into the turning point and slow down.

> "Watch the type here."

Let the kinetic moment land fully. The display face gains weight as the turning
point enters view. Do not rush past it. Then reach the shape of the week and the
close.

## Beat 4: the bigger point (1:45 to 2:05)

Tap share, show the link, and if time allows open it in a new tab to show the
personalized preview.

> "Every generated site is different, because every week actually is. And every one
> is a shareable link with its own preview card. This is what a model in the runtime
> loop looks like, not just a model before the demo."

## On camera honesty

If the live GPT-5.6 pass is running, say so. If you are showing a cached reading,
say that too. State plainly what is live and what is pre generated. It reads as
confidence, and the reading room looks identical either way.

## Backup plan

Before recording, generate one shared link and keep it open in a tab. If the live
pass stalls, cut to that finished site and narrate Beat 3 from there.
